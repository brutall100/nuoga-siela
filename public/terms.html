Nuoga Siela

Terms of use

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Nuoga Siela (nuogasiela.lt and the Android app) is an anonymous space to say what you feel. By publishing a text to the Stream or Stories you agree to these terms.

This is not professional help

Nuoga Siela is not a psychological, psychiatric or medical service and does not replace one. If your life or someone else's is in danger, call 112. If it's hard — Hope line 116 123 (24/7), Youth line 8 800 28888 (24/7), Lithuania.

Who can use it

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over.

What's welcome

Expressing your feelings — anger, sadness, anxiety, guilt, loneliness, hope. You can write openly and even swear: this is a space to let it out. Others can only tap “I understand you” — there are no comments.

What's not allowed

  • Information that identifies you or someone else: full names, addresses, schools or workplaces together with names, phone numbers, emails, social media accounts.
  • Accusing, defaming, threatening, stalking or humiliating specific people.
  • Hate based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or other characteristics.
  • Describing, encouraging or giving instructions for suicide or self-harm methods. You may talk about not wanting to live — not about methods.
  • Sexual content, especially anything involving minors (such content is reported to law enforcement).
  • Inviting people to chat or meet outside the app, advertising, spam.
  • Anything that breaks Lithuanian or EU law.

Moderation

Every text can be reported with the flag button. After several reports a text is hidden automatically. Reported and hidden texts are reviewed: those within the rules are restored, those breaking them are deleted permanently. You can also send rule violations or legal requests by email (the address is in the privacy policy, “Who we are”) — include a link to the text or an excerpt.

Since there are no accounts, a person can't be “banned” — instead, violating texts are removed and automatic limits restrict how much and how often anyone can post.

Your texts

Your texts remain yours. By publishing you allow them to be shown anonymously in the app: Stream texts disappear after 24 hours, Stories stay until you delete them and can be found by search engines (e.g. Google). You can delete your texts in settings (“Delete my data”). How data is handled — see the privacy policy.

Liability

Texts are written by users and reflect their own experience and opinions. The app is provided “as is”, without guarantees that it will always work or be error-free.

Changes

If these terms change, we'll update the date at the top of this page and announce significant changes in the app.
