Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Nuoga Siela (nuogasiela.lt) is an anonymous space to let things out. This policy explains what data we collect, what we don't, and why.

Who we are

Nuoga Siela is an independent, non-commercial project. Contact: aldas.kse@gmail.com.

No accounts, no identity

The app never asks for your name, email, phone number, or any other information that could identify you. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Device identifier

The first time you open the app, your browser generates a random code (crypto.randomUUID()) stored on your device. It is sent along with your actions (posting, "I understand you", reporting), and the server immediately turns it into an irreversible cryptographic hash (SHA-256 with a secret server-side salt). The original code is never stored or logged — only the hash is. It is not linked to your name, IP address or any other data; it is used only to show you your own texts, let you delete them, count "I understand you" and reports once per device, and limit how many texts can be posted.

In Settings ("Deletion key") you can view and save this code: with it you can delete your texts from another device too. Treat it like a password.

What happens to your text

  • "Burn it" — the text never leaves your device. It is processed entirely in the browser and is never sent to any server.
  • "Release" (Stream) — the text is sent to the server and shown to others with no author attribution. After 24 hours it is physically deleted from the database — not hidden, deleted.
  • "Keep as a story" (Stories) — the text stays visible indefinitely, until you delete it yourself from Settings ("Delete my data" or "Deletion key"). A story has its own public page that search engines index (Google and others); we notify Bing/Yandex of new stories via IndexNow (only the link is sent). A deleted story becomes unavailable immediately, but may remain in search results for a while until they refresh.

What we don't collect

  • IP addresses — the app doesn't store them, and its logs contain only the method, path and response status. The hosting provider (see "Sharing with third parties") processes IP addresses technically to deliver requests and protect against attacks, under its own privacy policy.
  • Name, email, phone number, or other contact details.
  • Tracking or advertising cookies. We use no third-party analytics or ad tools.
  • Precise geolocation.

Microphone — Scream Room

The Scream Room feature uses the microphone only so you can record and play back a sound for yourself. The recording is kept only in device memory (RAM) via the browser's MediaRecorder API — it is never sent to any server and never written to disk. Leaving the screen or closing the app irreversibly discards it.

Mood calendar

When you tag an emotion while writing, only the day and the emotion — never the text — are saved in your browser's storage (localStorage). This is never sent anywhere and the server never sees it; it is only used to draw your personal calendar.

For the shared weekly mood ("How everyone feels"), the server only counts how many times each emotion was tagged in released texts per day. These are plain totals with no link to any text, device or person, and they delete themselves after 8 days.

Notifications

Notifications about new stream texts are off until you turn them on in Settings. When on, the server stores only your browser's push address and keys, the chosen mode ("once a day" or "right away"), the language and when we last sent a notification. This is not linked to your device code or your texts in any way — we don't know who receives notifications or what they wrote.

A notification contains only the number of new texts, never the text itself. It is delivered by your browser's or phone's push service (on Android — Google Firebase Cloud Messaging); the content is encrypted, so the service cannot read it. Turning notifications off or tapping "Delete my data" deletes the subscription immediately; an unused subscription deletes itself after 90 days.

Crisis support (SOS)

If your text contains words related to suicide or self-harm, the text is still published — nothing is blocked. You are simply shown additional helpline information (Vilties linija 116 123, Jaunimo linija 8 800 28888 — Lithuanian national helplines). This detection does not store any text content linked to your identity.

Moderation

Texts can be reported. After several reports a text is hidden, and reported texts are reviewed by the project's author, who restores or deletes them. Review shows only what is public anyway (text, emotion, number of reports) — never the device hash. Rules — see the terms of use.

Data deletion

The "Delete my data" button in Settings immediately and permanently deletes all of your posted texts (feed and stories), your "I understand you" and report marks and your device marker from the server, and your mood calendar from this device. Temporary rate-limit counters expire on their own within 24 hours.

Legal basis and your rights

We process data based on our legitimate interest in providing this service and protecting it from abuse (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and notification subscriptions based on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw in Settings at any time. Your texts may reveal feelings or health, which is why we never link them to your identity.

You have the right to access, erase and restrict processing of your data and to object to processing. Since we can't identify you, you exercise these rights yourself in Settings or by emailing us your deletion key. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share any data with third parties for advertising or analytics purposes. The app runs on the Deno Deploy hosting platform (Deno Land Inc., USA) with no external tracking tools; data may be processed outside the EU under the provider's data transfer safeguards. If you turn on notifications, they are delivered through your browser's or phone's push service (see "Notifications"). Links to public stories are shared with search engines (see "What happens to your text").

Children's privacy

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at anyone under 16, and we deliberately avoid collecting any personally identifying data from users of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced within the app.
